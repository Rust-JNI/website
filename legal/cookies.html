Cookie Policy

Draft, last updated 2 September 2026. This is a template pending final legal review. It is not binding until published as final.

This page explains how rjni.dev uses cookies and similar storage.

1. What this site sets today

Nothing. rjni.dev is currently a set of static pages with no accounts, no server-side sessions, and no analytics or tracking of any kind. We do not set a single cookie today. This page exists so that changes, if any, get documented here first rather than silently.

2. What might be added later

When real checkout goes live (see our Terms of Service), our payment processor will very likely set its own strictly necessary cookies to run checkout securely: standard for any payment flow, and unrelated to tracking. If we ever add analytics, it will be listed here with what it collects and, where consent is legally required, a way to decline it. We do not run third-party advertising cookies, and we do not sell any data to third parties.

3. Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies through your browser's settings at any time.

4. Contact

Questions: privacy@rjni.dev. See also our Privacy Policy.
