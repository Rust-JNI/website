Privacy Policy

Draft, last updated 2 September 2026. This is a template pending final legal review. It is not binding until published as final.

This policy explains what data RJNI ("we", "us") collects through rjni.dev and the RJNI software (together, the "Service"), and why. RJNI is operated by a sole trader based in England, and this policy is written to comply with the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, and, where a customer is based in the EU, the EU GDPR, to the extent it applies to us.

1. What we collect

  • Account and purchase data: your email address, and payment details processed by our payment provider. We do not store full card numbers ourselves.
  • Licence activation data: a hashed device identifier per activation, used solely to enforce your plan's per-device activation limit and to let you deactivate a device. We treat a hashed device identifier as personal data, consistent with UK GDPR guidance that pseudonymised identifiers are still personal data when they can be linked back to an individual. This policy, and our retention practice, applies to it accordingly.
  • Support communications: anything you send us by email when requesting support.

We do not currently use any analytics or tracking cookies on this website. See our Cookie Policy for the current, short list of what this site actually sets. If that changes, this policy and the Cookie Policy will be updated first.

We do not collect, upload, or have any access to your source code, your compiled output, or the contents of any jar you process with the Software. Protection happens entirely on your own machine or build infrastructure.

2. Why we collect it, and our legal basis

  • To issue, validate, and let you manage your licence: necessary to perform our contract with you.
  • To process payment and provide receipts: necessary to perform our contract with you, and to meet our legal obligations.
  • To respond to support requests: our legitimate interest in running the Service, or performance of our contract with you.
  • To meet our own legal and accounting obligations: legal obligation.

3. Licence validation and offline use

The Software validates your licence against our server on each build, with a signed, cached validation token allowing continued use for a limited offline grace period if our server is unreachable. This check confirms licence validity. It does not transmit your code or output.

4. Retention

We retain account and licence data for as long as your licence is active, plus a reasonable period afterward for legal, accounting, and support purposes. You can request deletion of your account data at any time. See Section 6.

5. Sharing

We share data only with the service providers necessary to run the Service: our payment processor, our hosting provider, and our licensing platform, each bound to protect it, and never sold to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

6. Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these, email privacy@rjni.dev. If you are not satisfied with how we have handled your data, you also have the right to complain to the UK's data protection regulator, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk

7. International transfers

We are based in England. Some of the service providers we use to run the Service, for example a payment processor or hosting infrastructure, may be based outside the UK, including in the United States. Where that is the case, we rely on a UK adequacy regulation, an International Data Transfer Agreement, or the provider's own equivalent safeguard, so your data stays protected to UK GDPR standards wherever it is actually processed. We will name the specific providers here once our payment and licensing platform is finalised.

8. Changes to this policy

We will update the last-updated date above when this policy changes, and post material changes here before they take effect.

9. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: privacy@rjni.dev
